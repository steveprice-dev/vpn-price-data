# Native-currency price tracking

Sanitized USD offers plus genuine EUR-only plans from AirVPN, AzireVPN and
Mullvad. All 29 tracked providers remain represented. Other non-USD test-market
observations are excluded. Each price keeps its original currency, observation
time, source and evidence hash. Currency conversion is never performed.

`trend_eligible` requires a usable monthly duration. `upfront_eligible` separately
allows a verified upfront amount for short prepaid offers such as AirVPN's
three-day plan. Stale records are ineligible for either measure.

The USD-only feed and earlier monthly releases remain available unchanged for
reproducibility. Current monthly reports use this native-currency feed.
