# Automation eligibility

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

The daily panel is limited to public pages that can be collected without
contradicting an express provider restriction. Robots rules and provider terms
are separate checks: an `Allow` directive does not override a contractual
restriction. Rendering, proxies, alternate user agents, and manual copying are
not workarounds for a prohibited collection method.

| Provider | Daily status | Basis | Reconsideration requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| TorGuard | Excluded | Published terms prohibit automated or manual monitoring and copying without prior written permission. | Written research permission or official feed. |
| PrivadoVPN | Excluded | Published terms prohibit automated bots, scripts, scrapers, or similar tools except through expressly available APIs. | Express API or written research permission. |
| FastestVPN | Excluded | Published terms prohibit automated crawling, data mining, and scraping unless expressly permitted. | Written permission or official feed. |
| X-VPN | Excluded | Published terms prohibit automated website or service access without prior permission. | Written permission or official feed. |

Primary policy pages:

- https://torguard.net/terms.php
- https://privadovpn.com/terms-of-service/
- https://fastestvpn.com/terms-of-service
- https://xvpn.io/terms-service

X-VPN remains a valuable future candidate because its Everest protocol and
privacy positioning fit the protocol-diversity scope. It is not collected
until the permission requirement is satisfied.
