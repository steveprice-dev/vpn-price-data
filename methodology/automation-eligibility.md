# Automation eligibility

Last reviewed: 2026-08-09

The daily panel is limited to public pages that can be collected without
contradicting an express provider restriction, unless the provider has granted
a research exception covering the collection method. Robots rules and provider
terms are separate checks: an `Allow` directive does not override a contractual
restriction. Rendering, proxies, alternate user agents, and manual copying are
not workarounds for a prohibited collection method.

These exceptions change collection eligibility, not evidentiary quality. The
Action may retrieve and retain private evidence on test and scheduled runs.
Each provider is marked `permissioned_manual_review`; automated output remains
a draft until a researcher confirms plans, totals, free periods, renewal terms,
tax treatment, and source interpretation.
