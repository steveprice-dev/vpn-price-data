# Renewal and discount definitions

The first renewal is the first mandatory recurring charge after the
introductory service period, excluding optional add-ons. Both amount and period
must be recorded; `$99 renewal` is incomplete without the billed duration.

The study reports two different concepts:

- the provider's advertised discount percentage, reproduced as a claim; and
- the observed change from the provider's own pre-sale payable price, computed
  from dated observations.

“Free months” extend the service denominator only when the checkout evidence
shows they are included in the purchased term. They do not reduce the amount
paid immediately. A plan advertised as `$2/month` but charged as `$48 now` is
reported with both values.

A reference-price manipulation flag requires a dated increase in the stated
reference price without a matching change in the comparable ordinary payable
price. It is never inferred from one Black Friday screenshot.
